About

The person behind QAB.

Queen Ayodele Bezalel is an architect, executive ghostwriter, AI creative strategist, storyteller, and the founder of QAB.

Her work sits at the intersection of creativity, education, media, faith-rooted wisdom, professional insight, humour, identity, and legacy.

QAB is a media, education, and creative ecosystem for thoughtful people, hidden creatives, unfinished builders, and anyone who knows there is more to life than noise.
